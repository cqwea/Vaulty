# Vaulty

## About
A small money tracker for money savers which fetches the current rates and you type in your bought goods and title keep track of it and show you a portfolio that'll motivate you
## Features
1- İt can fetch currency rates online and update your savings to that
2-stores all the entries as history so you can see what you bought and what was the rates at that time being
3-Portfolio that you can see to get motivated of how much you've saved
4-Safe because no data leaves your device
